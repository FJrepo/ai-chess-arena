Troubleshooting

When OpenRouter is degraded

Test or replace the key in OpenRouter settings above. Operator-managed keys must be changed in their environment or secret file.

When Stockfish is degraded

Docker deployments should bundle Stockfish automatically. Source-based runs need a local stockfish binary on PATH.
